# Opeyemi Gadgets Concept

UNIVERSAL CLIENT WEBSITE CONCEPT & PRE-BUILD STRATEGY

Business: Opeyemi Phone & Gadgets
Registered CAC Business Name: BN 7710639
Contact: 09030393902
Services: Buy, Sell, Swap (New & UK Used Gadgets, Nationwide Delivery)

Approved specifications and confirmed decisions:

1. Developer Attribution
Use KELLYRAY / STUDIO as the development agency.
Footer credit: “Website Concept & Design by KELLYRAY / STUDIO”
Keep the attribution subtle, professional, and visually consistent with the website. Since this is a demonstration, clearly distinguish it from an officially commissioned website.

2. Pricing Currency
Use Nigerian Naira (₦) throughout the website.
Format amounts consistently using Nigerian currency conventions.
Only use actual prices from the supplied business materials. Where prices are unavailable, use “Request Price,” “Contact for Price,” or clearly labeled demonstration placeholders. Do not fabricate product prices, discounts, trade-in values, or swap quotations.

3. Product Catalog
Prioritize iPhones as the primary catalog category, with MacBooks and Apple Watches as supporting categories.
Use the supplied photos, inventory references, and business information to determine which products can be represented accurately.
Make the catalog easy to browse, filter, and navigate on mobile devices.
Do not claim that specific devices are in stock unless the supplied materials verify their availability.

4. Design & Conversion Priorities
Create a premium, modern, mobile-first electronics retail experience that communicates professionalism, product quality, and trust.
Prioritize:
- A strong, visually engaging homepage.
- Professional iPhone product listings.
- MacBook and Apple Watch collections.
- Clear device specifications (storage, condition grade, tested battery health range).
- A prominent WhatsApp inquiry CTA using the verified business number (09030393902).
- A phone-swap or trade-in inquiry experience ("We Buy, We Sell, We Swap").
- Responsive layouts and polished product presentation.
- Clear navigation and appropriate SEO foundations.

5. Demonstration Requirements
This project is intended to showcase development capabilities to the prospective business owner.
Do not activate real payments, place orders, submit customer data, or send automated WhatsApp messages during the demonstration.
Use only verified contact details and business information.
The website should look professional enough to demonstrate a potential production-ready experience while remaining clearly identifiable as an independent concept.

6. Implementation Authorization
Proceed with the approved demonstration build using the agreed strategy.
Use reusable, maintainable components and implement the functional interactions appropriate for a safe preview.
After building:
- Test responsive layouts.
- Check navigation and interactions.
- Identify missing assets or unverified information.
- Provide a working preview.
- Summarize completed features and remaining production requirements.
Do not connect a custom business domain or publish the website as the business’s official site without explicit authorization.
